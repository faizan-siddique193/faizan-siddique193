## Flagship AI Architecture: [AdAura](https://github.com/faizan-siddique193/AdAura) *(Active Development)*

**End-to-End Autonomous Ad-Tech & Marketing Automation SaaS for Fashion Enterprises**
An enterprise web platform that replaces fragmented marketing tools with a unified, stateful AI pipeline—handling everything from brand profiling to live campaign optimization.

- **Persistent Brand Context Engine:** Captures product catalogs, target demographics, language preferences, and budget constraints once, injecting this context across all downstream AI agents.
- **Automated Campaign Planning:** Generates data-driven media briefs detailing optimal ad channels, posting schedules, frequency targets, and cross-platform budget allocations.
- **Multimodal Content & Creative Generation:** Transforms raw product uploads into platform-sized promotional visuals alongside localized ad copy, product descriptions, and multi-variant CTAs.
- **Automated AI Quality Gate (Judge Agent):** Evaluates all generated creatives and copy against the initial campaign brief *before* user review, outputting a deterministic quality score and a targeted improvement recommendation per asset.
- **Direct Ad-Network Execution & Telemetry:** Publishes approved campaigns directly to connected ad networks (Meta API), aggregating live CTR, reach, impressions, engagement, and spend into a single control plane.
- **Anomaly Detection & Human-in-the-Loop Optimization:** Continuously monitors live telemetry to flag creative fatigue or performance drops, proposing actionable interventions (creative refresh, audience pivot, or budget reallocation) gated by one-click user approval.
- **Tiered Credit & Subscription Engine:** Manages monthly AI generation credits, active campaign slots, and linked ad-account quotas via Paddle billing.
- **Stack:** `Next.js` `FastAPI` `LangGraph` `Python` `Supabase` `Paddle`
